About me

From 2025 to 2026, Hong Chen was a Video Generation Algorithm Engineer on the Kling team at Kuaishou Technology, which he joined through the K-Star Talent Program. He worked on the pre-training of large-scale video generation foundation models, including the joint audio-video generation scheme of Kling 3.0 and the pre-training of Kling 4.0.

He received his Ph.D. from the Department of Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University in 2025, advised by Prof. Wenwu Zhu. His doctoral research focused on multimodal generative models, including customized text-to-image/text-to-video generation, disentangled representation learning, and multimodal large language models. He received his B.E. from the Department of Electronic Engineering, Tsinghua University in 2020.

His research interests include multimodal generative AI (video/image/audio generation, diffusion models), multimodal large language models, and curriculum/automated machine learning. He has published over 40 papers at venues such as NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, CVPR, KDD, ACM MM and IEEE TPAMI. According to Google Scholar, his work has received 2,900+ citations with an h-index of 27 (as of Sept. 2026).

Experience

  • Kuaishou Technology, Kling Team — Video Generation Algorithm Engineer, K-Star Talent Program (2025 - 2026)
    Pre-training of the Kling video generation foundation models:

    • Joint audio-video generation (Kling 3.0): designed and validated a multi-stream audio-video joint generation DiT architecture to address the quality gap caused by separately trained audio and video models. The scheme was shipped in Kling 3.0 with clear improvements over Kling 2.6.

    • DiT model scaling (Kling 4.0): designed and experimentally validated the MoE scaling scheme for DiT models, balancing efficiency and performance during scale-up. The scheme was adopted in the pre-training of Kling 4.0.

    • Inference algorithm: designed a dynamic classifier-free guidance (CFG) algorithm for multi-step audio-video generation, which keeps generation stable while reducing the "AI look" of generated videos.

  • Tencent, WeChat — Research Intern, Recommendation Group (2022)
    Behavior-aware cross-domain recommendation: automatic source-domain behavior selection with importance perception, validated in the industrial setting and published at WWW 2023.
